Method: % Short = inverse-ETF dollar volume ÷ total speculative-ETF dollar volume (leveraged-long + inverse), weekly. Dollar volume (shares × price) is used deliberately: raw share volume is dominated by ultra-low-priced inverse funds (e.g. TZA near $4 trades 300M+ shares), which distorts the ratio; dollar-weighting reproduces the 3Fourteen band. Data is refreshed server-side daily by a scheduled job and read from data.json — no browser-side market fetch, so it works reliably wherever it's hosted.
